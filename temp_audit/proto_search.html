View of Trust and behavioral intention toward blockchain-based mudharabah smart contracts in Islamic banking

Ahmad Fauzi, Siti Rahmawati, Budi Santoso

This study examines the factors influencing user trust and behavioral intention toward adopting blockchain-powered mudharabah smart contracts in modern Islamic banking institutions in Indonesia.
